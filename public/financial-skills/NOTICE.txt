The original workflow instructions and bundled reference files are reproduced
without edits inside each workflow JSON. The catalog descriptions, browser UI
and research brief adapter were added for Investment Studio.

Scope: 18 core financial-analysis and equity-research workflows.
This is a browser-based workflow and prompt workbench. It is not a deployment
of Claude Managed Agents or a connection to a financial data provider. It does
not execute scripts, run models, produce analyst reports or generate workbooks.
Those operations take place in the Claude session where a brief is run, subject
to the tools, subscriptions and data available in that session.

The research context entered in the workbench stays in the current browser tab
until the user copies or downloads it. Required workbooks and source documents
are attached separately in the destination session.
